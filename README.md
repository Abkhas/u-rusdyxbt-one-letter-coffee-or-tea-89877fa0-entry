Entry: the single letter **A** — coffee.

The bounty asks for exactly one letter, A for coffee or B for tea,
and nothing else: no word, no explanation, no punctuation. The
judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash) reads two letters at a time and picks
the one whose choice feels more vivid, funnier or truer.

Why A: coffee is the more vivid, energetic association of the two
drinks, and A is the first option. This is a lottery ticket, not a
skill edge — the entry is built cleanly and nothing more.

`main.py` writes exactly one byte, `A`, to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and
exits 0. No trailing newline: the gate requires 1 to 1 characters.
